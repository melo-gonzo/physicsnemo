**The principled case.**
- For ISLA-DTT: exact query independence. A prediction does not depend on which other points are queried, and
  QUERY-POP measures how much that matters to interacting ISLA.
- Against ISLA-DTT:
  - the canonical frame's axis sign on near-mirror-symmetric bodies (CANONICAL_SKEW_TIE and its lab-axis fallback),
    an open question;
  - its constants, and θ. θ's error vanishes as θ → 0, which the knob rule credits; DTT-KNOBS stage 2, training now,
    tests whether θ's error converges as θ tightens;
  - its 1-6 % movement under exact refinement;
  - on the tree these lanes ran, tree_read.py:178 assigns normal octants with `> 0`. That is not antipodal for zero
    components, so the forest partition is not flip-symmetric on axis-aligned faces. DTT-POSE has fixed it on its unmerged branch (above);
  - on AeroZoo, its float32 forward is not reproducible run to run on a few cases (the token draw; f32floor).
- ATTRIB-090's flip-invariance test of interacting ISLA is pending.

**AirfRANS context (as briefed; not part of the AeroZoo rule).** At 0.9.0 the bug-trained AirfRANS pair read interacting

## 5. Analyses beside the rules (post hoc)

- **The baselines** (`basecheck/`, with errata).
  - No pipeline bug was found.
  - Transolver is at its documented floor.
  - GeoTransolver's pressure is a dynamic-range floor: 8 decades of pressure RMS under one dataset-wide scale, with the
    case-relative loss.
  - Head-only AdamW does not lift it (`gtopt/`: best median 1.004 against the 0.9 bar), so its pressure is not
    optimizer-limited.
- **ISLA's pressure above 1** (`pdiag/`, `preddiag/`).
  - On crossflow, ISLA drives the flow about 2× too fast in roughly the right direction, and its pressure follows that
    overshoot: dividing by the squared speed ratio takes the median L2 from 3.3 to 0.33. It also does not hold p ≈ 0 at
    the adjacent outlet.
  - On nonbox it is a shape error by family.
- **Scales** (`scales/`, `nonbox_scales_final.md`).
  - On nonbox no test case lies below the training z (Re) range.
  - 121 ducts lie below the training range of the head velocity RMS, and their velocity error tracks that distance
    (Spearman +0.86 ISLA, +0.73 ISLA-DTT). That is an output-amplitude extrapolation, not the AirfRANS below-band Re
    pattern.
- **Reynolds split** (`reynolds/`). All test cases lie above the band, and ISLA's error grows ×1.23 (v) and ×1.67 (p)
  per decade. GeoTransolver is flat at its floor.
- **Compiled path** (`cmpdiag/`): not exposed.
- **Float32 floor** (`f32floor/`). It comes from the geometry: the wall-distance sign at folded features, and the
  in-model Morton token draw. It does not come from the blocks or from the coordinate magnitude, and the reviewer's
  near-wall hypothesis does not hold.
