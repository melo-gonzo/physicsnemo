**For the program.**
- **Which reads carry the model.** All of them. On AirfRANS the early reads (blocks 0-3) are large and the most damaging to cut; the late ones are small, aligned with the slice update, and mostly cheapest to cut. On lug the first two reads learned nothing.
- **Reading at fewer blocks.** B1 (every second block) beats keeping the last k at every k <= 6 (no more reads than B1), on both datasets, both seeds, zeroed and mean-replaced; the whole set tried k = 3 and 6. On the subset it also beats last 7, narrowly on lug (20.9/29.9 vs 21.2/32.2x), but not last 8 or 9 with their 8-9 reads (last 9 zeroed: 19.0 vs 35.1x on AirfRANS seed 42, 13.3/19.9 vs 20.9/29.9x on lug; mean-replaced last 9: 12.4 vs 17.5x on AirfRANS seed 42). Prediction: B1 is the stage-2 arm to run; last-k with k <= 6 will cost more. Inference cuts of a model trained with all reads cannot size the retrain cost.
- **A narrower read.** The basis is the clear slack on AirfRANS: basis 8 looks affordable there (12 is nearly free even without retraining). On lug basis 8 costs 2-4x at inference (1.9/3.6x; 2.1/6.6x on the principal directions), and the seeds differ by 2x. The 4-head cut is the uncertain half: energy sits in 4 heads, but cutting the other 4 costs 6-9x at inference. Prediction: C1's retrain cost, if any, comes mostly from the heads (on lug seed 43 the basis adds a factor 1.18 on top of the heads).
- **Cost.** On AirfRANS and lug the 12 per-block reads are a minor share of the eager step (B1 saves at most 12% of time, C1 at most 4%); this excludes the shared forest, walk and basis, which still ran. Stage-2 retrains there measure accuracy; the savings matter at car scale.
- **Confidence.** High on the structural statistics (the seeds agree). High on the arm ranking (B1 over last-k with k <= 6; basis before heads) as an inference ranking of models trained with all reads and all heads: it holds in both seeds, on the whole set and on the held-out cases, but its transfer to retrains is untested. Low on the per-block order, where the seeds disagree, on lug's basis cost, and on how large a retrain cost will be.
