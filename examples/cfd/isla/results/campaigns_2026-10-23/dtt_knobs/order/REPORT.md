**What it means for the program.** I am confident of these points:
- Do not plan an H1 Triton lane unless an eager retrain with H1, or with monopole logits plus the bounded value term, beats the monopole at theta >= 1. On these monopole-trained states full H1's median is worse than the monopole's at every theta >= 1 in every block (1.01-3.81x at theta 1) and its p95 at every theta >= 0.5, and H1 costs 2.9x the multiply-adds and 4.5x the node bytes.
- A trained ISLA-DTT reads far from the exact read at theta 1 (median up to 27% per block): the model is adapted to its own aggregate. Inference-only error budgets therefore do not price what a retrain at another theta would score. This lane ran no retrain; the direct evidence is E2 in the DTT row of the program ledger (`examples/cfd/isla/book/14-program-status.qmd` on origin/psharpe/isla d1bb2e887): checkpoints trained with the dense read missed E2's 1% bar at theta <= 0.5 by 2x to 20x, "so the model is trained at its theta". Here the converse holds: the exact read on the theta-1-trained model scores 1.9-7.2x worse (`prop_table.md`).
- A larger theta (A1) must be judged by retraining, not by this operator error.

Still open: whether a model trained with H1 keeps |q| times its in-cluster key spread small enough for the series to hold. Only training can say that, and the trained monopole model's states say no.
