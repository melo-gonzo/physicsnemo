- test_isla.py::test_interior_task_contracts (renamed, kept): its last assertion `pytest.raises(ValueError, match="interior=True")` for a surface model with `query_independent=True` became `pytest.raises(TypeError, match="unexpected keyword argument 'query_independent'")`: the argument no longer exists.
- test_isla_compile.py::test_whole_model_compiles_fullgraph_with_dynamic_shapes_and_matches_eager[interior-cpu] and [interior-cuda] — the interacting interior whole-model fullgraph compile (a token axis of three counts with prediction points); ISLA-DTT's boundary read breaks a fullgraph trace, and its per-block compile is covered in test_isla_kernels.py.
- test_isla_conditions.py::test_every_condition_s_parameters_train_even_when_it_is_absent[isla] — interacting interior; `[isla_dtt]` covers it (now unparametrized). Note: the deleted parameter was the only one whose outlet declared the rank-2 field (its tensor map's gradient with the outlet empty).

## Commit C3 (ISLA tests: the boundary read live in the interior contracts; the draw under compile restored)

- Restored as converted, not deleted (the deletion in commit (a) is undone): test_isla_compile.py::test_the_in_model_boundary_draw_compiles_once_across_the_budget, test_a_warm_compile_cache_reuses_the_graph_across_the_budget, test_the_in_model_draw_compiles_under_max_autotune_at_full_resolution (CUDA, 1.4 million cells, max-autotune-no-cudagraphs), with _budget_model (ISLA-DTT, boundary_tokens 60) and BUDGET_SIZES. ISLA-DTT's whole forward does not trace fullgraph, so they compile the model's `_case_inputs` (the in-model draw and the prediction points' geometry custom op) with fullgraph=True, dynamic=True and error_on_recompile on every case after the first, and compare every leaf of the drawn boundary tokens and of the point tokens with eager at `_assert_close` (the tolerance the model outputs had). The token count `min(cells, 60)` is asserted per case.
- `live(model, seed=0)` (test/experimental/models/isla/_meshes.py) seeds every kernel read's zero-initialized output projection (`out.weight = randn * 0.1`, its own torch.Generator). It is applied to the interior contract tests: test_isla.py (gradient[interior], test_interior_task_contracts, its bf16 model, geometric_scale[interior], boundary_scalars_reach_the_interior_prediction, frame_is_sampling_consistent, scalar_only_model_...), and through the model helpers of test_isla_global_inputs.py, test_isla_heads.py, test_isla_names.py, test_isla_nd.py, test_isla_scales.py and test_isla_conditions.py (a no-op on their surface models), plus test_isla_routing.py's interior model. New test test_isla.py::test_live_makes_the_boundary_read_contribute: a fresh read's projection is zero; under `live` the outputs move by more than 1e-3; zeroing the projection again gives the fresh outputs bitwise.
- Changed condition (no tolerance changed; for the supervisor's judgment): two refinement contracts fail with a live read at the default opening angle TREE_READ_THETA = 1.0, because the refined boundary has another forest and the Barnes-Hut read is an approximation. test_isla.py::test_interior_task_contracts' `refine(mesh)` assertion measured 1.4e-4 on p and 1.9e-3 on v at theta 1, and about 1e-15 at theta 0. test_isla_conditions.py::test_remeshing_one_condition_changes_no_interior_prediction[*] fails the same way. Both now run their refinement assertion with the exact read (`_tree_read_theta = 0.0`, as test_isla_scales.py's `_model` and the tree-read tests already do), at the unchanged atol 1e-10. In test_interior_task_contracts the other assertions (query independence, chunks, covariance, similarity, gradients, bf16) still run at the default theta.
- Not given `live` (they assert geometry channels, the draw, padding or validation, not a contract the read could hide): test_isla_interior_geometry.py, test_isla_query_encoding.py, test_isla_sampling.py, test_isla_surface_conditions.py, test_isla_token_padding.py, test_isla_inputs.py (its interior model only checks the dimension refusal).
